## 摘要
用于职业提升的技术工具以周为尺度发生变化，而关于这些工具的书面指南也以同样速度过时。大语言模型使得起草教程或课程大纲的成本很低，但大多数已部署的生成系统仍然只生成一次内容，从不根据受众实际如何使用该内容进行检验。本文描述了一种以类型化工作流图组织的智能体流水线，它将教学内容生产视为带有明确检查点的制造过程，而非一次单独的生成调用。该图由四类节点构成：确定性脚本、绑定固定评分标准的模型步骤、开放式智能体阶段和人工审批门，它们沿前向生产路径排列，并由一个反馈循环闭环，该循环根据帖子级互动遥测数据重写生成规则。内容生产分为两层：需求层由视频和社区来源构建，用于塑造主题选择和语域；权威层由官方文档构建，每一条已发布的论断都必须能够追溯到该层。在五个月的持续运行中，部署系统发布了26篇有文档记录的指南，每周约需1.5小时操作员时间；反馈循环识别出四种可测量的互动效应，这些效应成为后续运行的约束规则，其中一种钩子风格在其首个分析窗口内的表现达到基线的1.83倍。

## Abstract
Technology tools used for professional upskilling change on a timescale of weeks, and written guidance about them ages at the same pace. Large language models have made it inexpensive to draft a tutorial or a course outline, but most deployed generation systems still produce a piece of content once and never check it against what an audience actually does with it. This paper describes an agentic pipeline, organized as a typed workflow graph, that treats instructional content production as a manufacturing process with explicit checkpoints rather than a single generation call. Four node classes structure the graph: deterministic scripts, model steps bound to a fixed rubric, open-ended agent phases, and human approval gates, arranged along a forward production path and closed by a feedback cycle that rewrites generation rules from post level engagement telemetry. Production of content is split into two layers: a demand layer, built from video and community sources, that shapes topic selection and register, and an authority layer, built from official documentation, that every published claim must trace back to. Over five months of continuous operation, the deployed system published 26 documented guides at roughly 1.5 hours of operator time per week, and the feedback loop identified four measurable engagement effects that became binding rules for later runs, including a hook style that outperformed baseline by a factor of 1.83 in its first analysis window.
