## 摘要
我们评估了GPT-5.1根据手写大学数学答案及其评分生成的反馈与练习题的质量，并考察接收这些反馈是否能改善后续考试表现。我们使用了六次每周加分测验，分别来自英语队列（n=343）和荷兰语队列（n=236）。测验1和测验3采用交叉反馈设计；其余情况下，所有参与者均获得反馈。为评估反馈，六名评审使用六项标准（每项0至3分）对来自英语队列三项任务的157条反馈消息进行了评分。练习题获得高评价的比例最高（95.5%），而诊断具体性（41.1%）以及清晰度与语气（33.4%）获得高评价的比例较低。针对得分最低答案的反馈被评为可操作性较低，这表明这些学生可能需要教师提供更有针对性的指导。对相关考试题目的比较结果不一，在四项比较中经调整后均无统计学显著差异。更高的出勤率和反馈接触程度与更高的考试分数相关，但反馈的贡献无法与出勤和练习的贡献区分开来。我们的研究结果表明，将教师指导与自动反馈生成相结合的综合框架具有潜力，可大规模提供个性化学习支持。

## Abstract
We evaluate the quality of feedback and practice exercises generated by GPT-5.1 from handwritten university mathematics answers and their grades, and examine whether receiving this feedback improves subsequent exam performance. We use six weekly bonus tests in an English (n=343) and a Dutch (n=236) cohort. Tests 1 and 3 used a crossover feedback design; otherwise, all attendees received feedback. To evaluate the feedback, six reviewers assessed 157 messages from three English-cohort tasks using six criteria scored from 0 to 3. The practice exercises received the highest proportion of strong ratings (95.5%), while diagnostic specificity (41.1%) and clarity and tone (33.4%) received fewer. Feedback for the lowest-scoring answers was rated as less actionable, suggesting that these students may need more targeted guidance from instructors. Comparisons of related exam questions showed mixed results, with no statistically significant differences after adjustment across four comparisons. Greater attendance and feedback exposure were associated with higher exam scores, but the contribution of feedback could not be separated from attendance and practice. Our findings show the potential of an integrated framework merging instructor guidance with automated feedback generation to provide personalised learning support at scale.
