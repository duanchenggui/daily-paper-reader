Abstract —During independent home study sessions, students frequently sustain improper sitting postures, accumulate excessive uninterrupted sedentary hours, and experience productivity loss caused by off-task digital media distractions. Furthermore, learners commonly lack real-time pedagogical scaffolding when encountering demanding self-study curricula. To mitigate these interrelated ergonomic and instructional challenges, this paper presents an affordable, privacy-preserving cyber-physical em-bedded system designed specifically for domestic study spaces. Engineered on a single-board computer (Raspberry Pi 3 Model B, 1 GB RAM) paired with a UVC optical camera and a USB condenser microphone, the system executes on-device computer vision utilizing lightweight landmark inference (MediaPipe Pose) and OpenCV to monitor upper-body spatial alignment, sagittal head inclination, and coronal shoulder asymmetry in real time without persisting raw video streams. A deterministic session management finite state machine (FSM) quantifies active study durations, enforces health-compliant rest periods, and detects illicit smartphone usage. Simultaneously, an integrated multimodal artificial intelligence tutoring agent parses voice and text queries via an encrypted API gateway, synthesizing Socratic conceptual hints rather than direct solutions. A lightweight parental web dashboard delivers encrypted session analytics and scheduling configurations. Empirical benchmarks executed on the physical prototype confirm an end-to-end inference latency of 244 –250 ms at a 352 × 288 analysis resolution (sustaining 4.0–4.1 inference frames per second), a trunk-angle estimation error of 4.39 ◦ mean absolute error over 5760 synthetic ground-truth poses, a resident set size of 320 MB, a peak SoC temperature of 58 .0◦C under sustained load, and a total latency of 1.58 s for the offline TTS feedback path, proving high practicality for resource-constrained edge deployment. 

Index Terms —Embedded Systems, Ergonomic Posture Moni-toring, Edge Computing, Computer Vision, MediaPipe Pose, AI Tutor, Socratic Scaffolding, Raspberry Pi. 

I. I NTRODUCTION 

The rapid proliferation of digital pedagogical curricula, remote learning platforms, and self-directed academic en-vironments has fundamentally shifted the locus of primary and secondary education into the home. While digital tools democratize access to academic information, independent study without continuous ergonomic and supervisory oversight introduces severe physical and cognitive vulnerabilities for adolescent students. From a physiological perspective, prolonged seated screen interaction frequently induces musculoskeletal disorders. Sus-tained static flexion of the cervical spine—commonly termed “text neck” syndrome—magnifies compressive forces on cer-vical intervertebral discs, while slouched thoracic kyphosis and asymmetrical lateral pelvic loading induce chronic lower back strain and visual fatigue. Epidemiological assessments confirm that sedentary behavior exceeding uninterrupted 45-minute intervals impairs circulatory dynamics and degrades sustained cognitive attention. Concurrently, unmonitored digital home environments expose students to cognitive fragmentation caused by non-educational mobile devices, gaming applications, and social media notifications. From a supervisory and academic standpoint, modern working parents cannot provide uninterrupted observation during after-school study sessions. Manual parental spot-checks often precipitate familial tension and fail to deliver objective empirical metrics regarding genuine study engagement, break compliance, and ergonomic posture health. Furthermore, when students confront complex mathematical derivations or sci-entific concepts independently, existing commercial search utilities and generative language models often output direct answers immediately. This direct answer delivery short-circuits cognitive struggle, cultivating academic dependency rather than reinforcing analytical problem-solving abilities. To resolve these interconnected ergonomic, supervisory, and instructional challenges, this paper develops an integrated, low-cost cyber-physical system tailored for home learning envi-ronments. The core framework couples localized edge vision inference on an embedded single-board computer (Raspberry Pi 3 Model B) with an interactive, cloud-orchestrated multimodal artificial intelligence tutoring agent. The primary contributions of this paper are summarized as follows:  

> •

Privacy-Preserving Edge Vision Pipeline: A lightweight, on-device pose estimation architecture that evaluates multi-planar ergonomic indices (sagittal trunk flexion, arXiv:2611.34669v1 [cs.AR] 25 Nov 2026 craniovertebral neck tilt, and coronal shoulder asymmetry) in real time while guaranteeing complete video privacy through volatile, in-memory processing.  

> •

Deterministic Ergonomic Session Manager: A fi-nite state machine (FSM) that automatically tracks net academic engagement, coordinates periodic rest breaks aligned with ergonomic health guidelines, and flags off-task mobile screen distractions.  

> •

Socratic Multimodal AI Tutoring Agent: A hybrid conversational agent capable of ingesting speech and text, utilizing structured system prompts to provide step-by-step conceptual scaffolding rather than ready-made solutions, with automatic model-fallback resilience against cloud-side transient failures.  

> •

Comprehensive Embedded Benchmarking: Extensive empirical validation on resource-limited ARM architecture profiling frame rates, geometric estimation accuracy, memory footprint, and thermal dissipation under sustained real-time load on the deployed prototype. II. R ELATED WORK 

A. Ergonomic Posture Tracking Technologies 

Ergonomic monitoring systems traditionally relied on wear-able sensors, including chest straps, electromyography (EMG) patches, and multi-axis Inertial Measurement Units (IMUs) affixed to the spine and cervical vertebrae. While IMUs yield precise kinematic angle measurements, wearable hardware intro-duces significant practical limitations for school-aged children: physical discomfort, skin irritation, recurring calibration drift, and the persistent burden of battery recharging. Consequently, vision-based non-intrusive posture estimation has garnered widespread research interest. Deep convolutional neural network (CNN) architectures such as OpenPose deliver robust multi-person joint coordinates but impose computational demands exceeding the hardware capabilities of standalone edge microprocessors. The introduction of optimized two-stage regression pipelines—notably Google MediaPipe Pose [1]— enables single-person landmark inference on commodity CPU threads. However, deploying such pipelines on embedded pro-cessors like the Broadcom BCM2837 requires strict resolution optimization, algorithmic pipelining, and temporal smoothing to suppress high-frequency spatial jitter. Our deployment quantifies this trade-off empirically: the MediaPipe Pose Lite landmarker exhibits a resolution-independent inference cost of 244 –250 ms per frame on the Cortex-A53, meaning that resolution reduction of the analysis stage—not the capture stage—is the only effective lever for latency reduction. 

B. Sedentary Behavior Guidelines and Focus Management 

The World Health Organization (WHO) and ergonomic bodies emphasize that mitigating sedentary risks requires regular postural alternations and periodic physical activity intervals [2]. Popular digital time management utilities, such as standalone Pomodoro timer applications, depend exclusively on manual self-reporting and lack physical validation of user presence. Prior embedded attempts incorporating ultrasonic or passive infrared (PIR) proximity sensors confirm physical occupancy but remain incapable of discerning whether the learner is engaged in academic tasks, slouching hazardously, or actively distracted by handheld smartphones. 

C. Multimodal Intelligent Tutoring Systems (ITS) 

Intelligent Tutoring Systems have progressed from early rule-based expert systems to contemporary Large Multimodal Models (LMMs). Standard generative AI interfaces, however, tend to hallucinate unverified shortcuts or immediately display finalized answer keys, undermining student perseverance. Pedagogical research proves that Socratic scaffolding—wherein the tutor provides leading clues, clarifies prerequisite axioms, and prompts reflective self-correction—fosters superior con-ceptual retention. Furthermore, hosting high-parameter vision-language models locally on low-cost edge platforms remains computationally unfeasible due to strict RAM and throughput bounds, necessitating a hybrid edge-cloud partitioning scheme. The FSM comprises four explicit operating modes: 1) Active Study State: Landmark detection confirms user presence, posture angles remain within compliant ranges, and no disallowed objects are detected. The net focus timer increments monotonically. 2) Rest Break State: Triggered after 45 minutes of accu-mulated study time. The system sounds an audible chime and mutes ergonomic alerts for 5 minutes, prompting physical stretches. 3) Away/Inactive State: Initiated when keypoint tracking is lost for longer than ∆taway = 60 seconds, pausing timers to prevent distorted study metrics. 4) Off-Task Warning State: Driven by an EfficientDet-Lite0 object detector executing in a dedicated background thread on subsampled frames (1 frame every 3 seconds). If a smartphone bounding box overlaps the hand-head interaction zone for ∆tdistract > 10 seconds, an alert sounds and off-task duration accumulates. 

D. Asynchronous Detection and Memory-Bounded Inference 

Running the object detector synchronously on the main inference thread is prohibitively expensive on the Cortex-A53. We therefore decouple the two neural workloads: the MediaPipe Pose landmarker executes in the primary vision thread, while EfficientDet-Lite0 runs inside a dedicated daemon thread that consumes only the most recent submitted RGB frame (an overwrite-on-write single-slot queue). The main loop reads the latest available detection result opportunistically, decoupling detection latency from the posture loop entirely. Measured on the deployed prototype, this asynchronous decoupling reduced the effective end-to-end inference latency from 1100 ms to 

290 ms and raised the sustained vision frame rate from 1.2 to 

2.6 frames per second in the full production pipeline. Memory discipline on a 1 GiB platform is equally critical. The hand-landmark sub-model ( 77 MB resident) is instantiated 

lazily , only after a smartphone bounding box is confirmed at a confidence level exceeding the production threshold of 0.55 ;candidate detections below this threshold are rendered as amber bounding boxes without invoking the model. Furthermore, the model is explicitly released once no smartphone candidate has been observed for 120 seconds. Measured resident set size of the complete application process is 320 MB with 230 MiB of system memory remaining available under sustained operation. 

E. Multimodal Socratic AI Tutoring Agent 

To deliver rigorous academic assistance without generating unsolicited direct answers, the interactive tutoring subsystem employs a hybrid edge-cloud pipeline: 1) Acoustic Query Digitization: The learner activates voice input by uttering the wake-word “assistant voice”. The captured audio is streamed to a cloud ASR engine and converted to text. 2) Context Packaging: The transcribed query is bundled with the current session context (accumulated study time, recent posture alerts, task list) into a compact textual payload. 3) Pedagogical Prompt Orchestration: The payload is packaged with an enforced Socratic system prompt and sent to the Gemini API over TLS:              

> “Tro ly hoc tap -Socratic Vietnamese tutor. Answer accurately, concisely, encourage self-learning; if unsure, clarify.”

4) Resilient Model Dispatch: Because the deployed API key encountered genuine transient cloud conditions— HTTP 503 (sustained high demand on the primary model) and HTTP 429 (quota exhaustion)—the dispatcher implements an ordered fallback chain across four Flash-class variants (3.8 → 3.7 → 3.6 → 3.5), retrying with exponential back-off before descending the chain. This design decouples the tutoring subsystem from any single point of failure. 5) Acoustic Delivery: The returned pedagogical hint is vo-calized through the HDMI output using the edge-tts 

cloud synthesizer, with a fully offline espeak-ng

V. P RIVACY , S ECURITY , AND ETHICAL CONSIDERATIONS 

The deployment of optical cameras in private domestic environments necessitates strict privacy-by-design guarantees:  

> •

Ephemeral Video Frame Lifecycle: Optical video buffers are retained exclusively within volatile RAM and overwritten after landmark extraction. No raw images or video streams are written to disk storage or transmitted externally.  

> •

Opt-In Academic Ingestion: Transmission to cloud endpoints occurs only upon explicit wake-word initiation by the learner, carrying only the transcribed query and compact session aggregates.  

> •

Zero-Biometric Parental Summaries: The local web dashboard records only non-biometric numerical aggre-gates (e.g., net study minutes, hourly slouch incidents, break adherence percentages), safeguarding adolescent psychological autonomy.  

> •

Encrypted Transport: All outbound API telemetry transmits through TLS tunnels with standard certificate verification, preventing localized network eavesdropping. API credentials are stored locally and are redacted in all diagnostic surfaces.  

> •

On-Device Operation Degradation: Because all pos-ture inference is fully local, the ergonomic monitoring subsystem—the safety-critical component—continues to operate without network connectivity. VI. C ONCLUSION AND FUTURE WORK 

This paper presented an integrated, low-cost embedded ergonomic posture monitoring and AI-assisted home learning system. By executing real-time lightweight computer vision on a single-board computer (Raspberry Pi 3 Model B, 1 GB RAM), the system successfully evaluates upper-body sitting ergonomics, enforces structured sedentary breaks, and detects off-task device usage while preserving household privacy. Concurrently, an on-demand AI agent provides Socratic peda-gogical scaffolding for complex study problems, with a multi-model fallback chain ensuring continuity under adverse cloud conditions. Empirical benchmarks on the physical prototype demonstrate 4.0–4.1 FPS landmark inference at 244 –250 ms per frame, a trunk-angle estimation error of 4.39 ◦ MAE, a 320 MB application footprint, a peak SoC temperature of 58 .0◦C over an 86 -minute soak test, and a 1.58 s offline TTS turnaround. Future extensions will explore the integration of dedicated edge Neural Processing Units (NPUs), such as the Raspberry Pi AI Kit, to support local execution of quantized Small Vision-Language Models (e.g., Moondream, SmolVLM), eliminating cloud API dependence and delivering a fully self-contained edge learning appliance. A further avenue is the extension of the geometric metric engine with a formally validated multi-session dataset annotated by goniometric reference, enabling the reporting of empirical precision, recall, and confusion matrices across the full ergonomic taxonomy rather than geometric reconstruction error alone.
