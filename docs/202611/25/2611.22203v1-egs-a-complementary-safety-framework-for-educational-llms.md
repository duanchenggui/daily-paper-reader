## 摘要
大语言模型（LLMs）在个性化教育方面具有巨大潜力，但面向青少年的部署会带来有毒、有偏见、误导性或不符合其发展阶段的内容风险。我们认为，教育安全不应局限于二元式拒绝：学生查询往往含糊不清或由好奇心驱动，需要情境化引导而非简单阻断。我们提出教育护栏系统（EGS），这是一个轻量级、模型无关的运行时框架，通过查询审核、场景感知的教学引导和回复审核来补充内在对齐。EGS 按风险等级对输入进行分诊，利用类别特定的教育干预重定向灰色地带查询，并在输出交付前进行审核。在通用基准和教育特定基准上的实验表明，EGS 在多种 LLM 上提升了审核性能、拒绝质量和越狱鲁棒性。这些发现表明，纵深防御式护栏可以增强安全性，同时保留教育 LLMs 所需的教学效用。我们的代码和模型权重将公开提供，以支持社区采用。

## Abstract
Large Language Models (LLMs) offer strong potential for personalized education, but adolescent-facing deployment raises risks of toxic, biased, misleading, or developmentally inappropriate content. We argue that educational safety should move beyond binary refusal: student queries are often ambiguous or curiosity-driven, requiring contextual guidance rather than simple blocking. We introduce the Educational Guardrail System (EGS), a lightweight, model-agnostic runtime framework that complements intrinsic alignment through query moderation, scenario-aware pedagogical guidance, and response moderation. EGS triages inputs by risk level, redirects gray-zone queries with category-specific educational interventions, and audits outputs before delivery. Experiments across general and education-specific benchmarks show that EGS improves moderation performance, refusal quality, and jailbreak robustness across diverse LLMs. These findings suggest that defense-in-depth guardrails can strengthen safety while preserving the pedagogical utility required in educational LLMs. Our code and model weights will be publicly available to support community adoption.
