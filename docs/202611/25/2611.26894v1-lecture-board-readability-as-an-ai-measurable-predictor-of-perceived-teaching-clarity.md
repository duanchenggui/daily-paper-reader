## 摘要
板书在物理和数学的大学教学中仍处于核心地位，但教师所写内容的视觉质量很少得到系统测量。我们考察了视觉语言模型能否以与学生感知教学方式相关的方式对板书进行评分。我们分析了由18名讲师讲授的56门大学物理和应用数学课程中的2,645张板书图像，并使用Claude 3.5 Sonnet对每张图像在可读性、组织性和视觉元素方面各评分50次。课程层面的可读性与学生评定的清晰度（r = .43）和组织性（r = .46）相关，并且在控制班级规模、课程平均成绩和学年后，仍是总体调查评分的唯一显著预测因子。这些关联对按讲师聚类具有稳健性，并且在每堂课仅使用一到两张图像时仍保持稳定。在由四名人工评分者对100张图像评分的子样本中，对于视觉元素，AI与人工评分之间的一致性可与评分者自身之间的一致性相当；对于可读性较低；对于组织性最弱。学生评价作为感知清晰度的基准，而非整体教学质量的基准。板书图像的自动分析可能为STEM教学提供一种低成本、可扩展的形成性反馈来源。

## Abstract
Lecture boards remain central to university teaching in physics and mathematics, yet the visual quality of what instructors write has rarely been measured systematically. We examined whether a vision-language model can score board writing in a way that relates to how students perceive instruction. We analysed 2,645 board images from 56 university physics and applied mathematics courses taught by 18 lecturers, scoring each image 50 times with Claude 3.5 Sonnet on readability, organization and visual elements. Course-level readability was associated with student-rated clarity ($r$ = .43) and organization ($r$ = .46), and remained the only significant predictor of the overall survey score after controlling for class size, mean course grade and study year. These associations were robust to clustering by lecturer and remained stable when only one or two images per lecture were used. In a subsample of 100 images rated by four human raters, agreement between AI and human scores was comparable to agreement among the raters themselves for visual elements, lower for readability and weakest for organization. Student evaluations served as a benchmark of perceived clarity rather than of teaching quality as a whole. Automated analysis of board images may offer a low-cost, scalable source of formative feedback in STEM teaching.
