## 摘要
本项工作进展（WIP）研究—实践研究考察了利用学生创建的作品来促进大规模本科软件设计与工程（SWE）课程中的主动学习。已有研究表明，主动学习策略能够显著提升学生的参与度、理解力和学习动机，在计算教育中尤其如此。在本研究中，我们探索利用学生创建的作品来促进学生参与高年级计算机科学（CS）课程。具体而言，我们考察了参与创建两类作品的计算机科学（CS）专业本科生和研究生的体验：教学视频教程与对话式人工智能代理。目标是理解设计这些作品如何支持学生应用和学习课程内容。学生就这两类体验提出了有价值的见解。关于教学视频的创建，他们认可多媒体为其学习体验带来的互动性，尽管部分学生认为该策略较为耗时。同样，在设计对话式人工智能代理时，学生喜欢任务的新颖性和实际应用性，而部分学生则遇到了与 Google Dialogflow ES 等工具复杂性相关的挑战。我们报告了学生创建的作品在促进对课程材料更深入参与方面的潜力，并在计算教育中鼓励创造力和批判性思维。

## Abstract
This work-in-progress (WIP) research-to-practice study examines the use of student-created artifacts to promote active learning in a large undergraduate Software Design and Engineering (SWE) course. Active learning strategies have been shown to significantly enhance student engagement, comprehension, and motivation, particularly in computing education. In this work, we explore the use of student-created artifacts to foster student engagement in higher-level Computer Science (CS) courses. Specifically, we examine the experiences of undergraduate and graduate CS students who participated in the creation of two types of artifacts: instructional video tutorials and conversational AI agents. The goal being to understand how designing these artifacts could support students in applying and learning course content. Students raised valuable insights regarding both experiences. Concerning the creation of instructional videos, they appreciated the interactivity brought through multimedia to their learning experiences, although some found the strategy to be time-consuming. Similarly, when designing conversational AI agents, students enjoyed the novelty and practical application of the task, while some encountered challenges related to the complexity of tools such as \textit{Google Dialogflow ES}. We report on the potential of student-created artifacts to foster deeper engagement with course material, encouraging both creativity and critical thinking in computing education.
